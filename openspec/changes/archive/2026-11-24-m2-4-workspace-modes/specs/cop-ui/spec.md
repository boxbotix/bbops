## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Task-modal 4-workspace shell, invariant safety topbar, and Fleet Filmstrip
The `ui/src/routes/+page.svelte` shell, `ui/src/lib/stores/viewport.svelte.ts`, and workspace modules (`COP`, `Pilot`, `Bench`, `Cortex`) SHALL separate persona-specific stage layouts (`cop`, `pilot`, `bench`, `cortex`) and `Pilot` sub-layouts (`map` default vs. `split`) over shared live `telemetryStore` and `skgStore` connections while keeping global fleet fault counts, selected-entity `ControlAuthority`/`armed`/`LinkTier` badges (or isolated external-contact provenance), and a persistent `FleetFilmstrip` (`1`–`9` selection) non-modal across all workspaces, synchronizing workspace/layout/chrome state deterministically via URL hash fragments (`#...`) without regressing default `#sel=...` links or `20 Hz` zero-DOM-thrash invariants.

#### Scenario: Switching across COP, Pilot, Bench, and Cortex preserves live streams, selection, and non-modal fleet fault visibility
- **WHEN** `skgStore` holds multiple ownship UXS vehicles (including an unselected vehicle in `P4 McuFailsafe` or `poisoned` state) and an external COP contact while the operator switches across `cop`, `pilot`, `bench`, and `cortex` (and `pilotLayout` `map` vs. `split` or `lightsOut` chrome) via controller verbs, hotkeys (`G`, `D`, `B`, `K`, `M`, `Tab`, `1`–`9`), or URL hash hydration
- **THEN** WebSocket #1 and WebSocket #2 remain continuously connected without reconnect churn, the invariant topbar continues surfacing the unselected vehicle's active fleet fault count alongside the selected entity's safety or external-contact summary, `FleetFilmstrip` updates selection deterministically, and `ViewportSnapshot` round-trips workspace, `pilotLayout`, and `lightsOut` state losslessly through `#...` URL hashes (falling back to `"cop"`, `"map"`, and `false` on omitted or malformed tokens)

#### Scenario: Pilot Map-Primary swap and COP Solo-Mode rail enforce external-contact isolation and scroll-free focus
- **WHEN** `COP` switches right-rail sections (`summary`, `alerts`, `skg`) or `Pilot` toggles `[M]` between `Map-Primary` (chase-capable `TacticalCanvas` with floating corner `VehicleHud` PiP) and `50/50 Split` across both a `VehicleEntityState` with abnormal `HealthFlags` and an `ExternalContactEntityState`
- **THEN** `COP` expands strictly one Solo-Mode accordion panel at a time, `Pilot` promotes abnormal vehicle health chips (`severity !== "nominal"`) while rendering guarded action slots exclusively for commandable UXS vehicles, and selecting an `ExternalContactEntityState` withholds all `P1`–`P4` `ControlAuthority`, `armed`, and guarded actuation surfaces

#### Scenario: Bench 20 Hz telemetry ring buffer and staged tuning rack operate without per-frame Svelte reactivity thrash
- **WHEN** `Bench` receives rapid `20 Hz` `WS #1` `VehicleKinematics` frames across `sysid`s (including out-of-order timestamps) and an engineer adjusts staged tuning dial/slider values before reverting or applying
- **THEN** the bounded `300`-sample per-`sysid` telemetry ring buffer records monotonically newer kinematic samples for `rAF` 2D stripchart rendering without unbounded memory growth or `20 Hz` DOM `$state` invalidation, renders `0xB1` MCU/trunk timing counters (`loop_dt_us`, `max_loop_dt_us`, `rtt_us`, `crc_errors`, `HealthFlags`) and Lane 3 transition events, and tracks staged parameter diffs locally without transmitting unconfirmed packets on slider drag

#### Scenario: Cortex executes lexical, semantic, and neural SKG queries with a non-safety causal evidence subgraph and triple citations
- **WHEN** an analyst queries `Cortex` via `POST /graph-gateway/search` (`globalSearch`, `localSearch`, `semantic`, `similar`) and `POST /graph-gateway/graphql` (`pathSearch`) across UXS vehicles, external COP contacts, and multi-hop relationships (including when `embedding_enabled` is `false`)
- **THEN** `Cortex` renders ranked lexical/predicate matches (and surfaces `not_enabled` neural status cleanly without throwing when embeddings are disabled), projects the matched 2-hop `pathSearch` neighborhood into a focused causal evidence subgraph using strictly non-safety palette tokens (`red`, `amber`, `green` reserved for vehicle safety badges), cites grounding `SkgTriple` assertions with `indexedRevision`/`kvRevision` metadata, and exposes zero vehicle actuation affordances

## Context
- `ui/src/lib/viewport/hotkeys.ts:28,64-80,161-167,226-230` intercepts unmodified `Tab` whenever `isViewportScopeTarget(event.target)` matches `data-viewport-hotkey-scope="true"` (`TacticalCanvas.svelte:766,865-866`, `BenchWorkspace.svelte:470-472`, `CortexWorkspace.svelte:749-755`) and calls `preventDefault()`, trapping forward `Tab` focus on the canvas while `Shift+Tab` (`hotkeys.ts:121`) moves backward. `git log -S 'VIEWPORT_HOTKEY_SCOPE_ATTR'` (`d8573f8`) confirms `VIEWPORT_HOTKEY_SCOPE_ATTR` and `isViewportScopeTarget` have no other callers.
- `ui/src/routes/+page.svelte:45-62` wires `window` `keydown` to `handleViewportHotkey` (`G`, `D`, `B`, `K`, `M`, `1`–`9`, `T`, `C`, `H`, `Escape` in `hotkeys.ts:133-221`) with no operator disable mechanism; `isEditableHotkeyTarget` (`hotkeys.ts:30,40-62`) checks `INPUT`/`TEXTAREA`/`SELECT`/`contenteditable` but not ARIA widget roles (`textbox`, `searchbox`, `combobox`, `spinbutton`, `slider`).
- Shortcut hints are hardcoded in `WorkspaceShell.svelte:227,239` (`<kbd>G/D/B/K</kbd>`, `Lights-Out <kbd>Tab</kbd>`), `CopWorkspace.svelte:385,392,399,462,548` (`[D]`, `[B]`, `[K]`), `PilotWorkspace.svelte:202` (`[M]`), `TacticalCanvas.svelte:937,950` (`[T]`, `[C]`), and `FleetFilmstrip.svelte:50-52` (`<kbd>1..9</kbd>`).
- `CopWorkspace.svelte:292-330,333-561` renders `role="tablist"` with three `role="tab"` buttons lacking roving `tabindex`, `id`/`aria-controls`, arrow-key handling, `role="tabpanel"`/`aria-labelledby`, and focus recovery when panel children unmount.
- `WorkspaceShell.svelte:215-230,244-280` marks workspace buttons with `aria-pressed` instead of `aria-current="page"`, unmounts cross-workspace `.jump-btn` triggers (`CopWorkspace.svelte:380-400,457-464,543-549`) leaving focus on `document.body`, `CortexWorkspace.svelte:606-620` omits `aria-pressed` on `.tier-btn` controls, and `ui/src/styles/colors.css:32,57-79` defines `--ui-focus-ring: #38bdf8` without `:focus-visible` rules.

## Goals / Non-Goals
- **Goals:** Restore native `Tab`/`Shift+Tab` traversal everywhere (including after pointer focus on canvases); provide a non-conflicting `Lights-Out` shortcut and button; implement WAI-ARIA single-stop `tablist` + `tabpanel` semantics and focus recovery in `Fleet`; let operators disable global character-only shortcuts with synchronized UI hints/labels; guarantee programmatic active-workspace state, predictable post-switch focus, and unobstructed `:focus-visible` rings.
- **Non-Goals:** Custom keybinding remapper, full screen-reader audit certification, responsive drawer layout (`#42`), draft continuity (`#40`), or `M3.1` actuation (`#11`).

## Decisions

### D1: Non-conflicting replacement shortcut & scope cleanup for `Lights-Out`

### D2: Global character-key shortcut disable control, persistence, & hint sync (WCAG 2.1.4)
